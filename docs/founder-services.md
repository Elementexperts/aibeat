# Founder Services

## Positioning

AIBeat helps founders present AI products to readers who are actively exploring tools, launches, and AI workflows.

## Current Routes

- `/submit`: existing submission form and Resend-backed API.
- `/launch`: launch service overview.
- `/spotlight`: premium placement and editorial opportunity overview.
- `/advertise`: existing advertising inquiry page.
- `/partners`: partnership overview.
- `/claim`: manual listing claim guidance.

## Rules

- Sponsored and partner content must be clearly labeled.
- Spotlight does not guarantee traffic, sales, leads, rankings, or editorial endorsement.
- Claim requests do not grant instant edit access.
- Use domain email or manual review for listing ownership.

## Future Enhancements

- Step-based submission form.
- Launch application form.
- Claim request status.
- Founder profile and media kit intake.
- Reviewed-leads CSV workflow for outreach automation.
