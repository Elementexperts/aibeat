## What should be simplified

Directory-only card hierarchy/readability and category entry consistency, reusing the existing URL contract and components. Free founder intake can eventually adopt selected five-field local work after storage/abuse/editorial gates; keep legacy paid compatibility. Prefer safe receipt/next steps and minimal internal review over a founder dashboard. Commercial package comparisons/CTAs and sponsored labels may be clarified later without price/checkout changes.

No simplification should invent missing functionality: the Save button has no persistence, /launches is a featured collection rather than competition, current popularity is a heuristic and includes featuredBoost +8, and a submission reference is not a private status credential. Plain, accurate product labels are preferable to simulated community activity.
