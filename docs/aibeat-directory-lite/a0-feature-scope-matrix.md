## 4. Community prerequisites (design only)

One authenticated vote per user/tool needs a durable unique key, server-derived user identity, confirmed/eligible account, published product ID/slug mapping, immutable actor/product fields and atomic idempotent cast/unvote. Never accept client totals/timestamps/roles. Decide self-votes, confirmed-email changes, suspension, brigading/account age and bounded rates; email verification alone is not Sybil resistance. Preserve first-cast eligibility on remove/recast to prevent refreshing a period artificially; explicit correction/removal and retention policies need approval.

Weekly/monthly rankings require approved timezone/window boundaries (prior C Phase0 design proposes UTC; not adopted automatically), immutable timestamps, eligible distinct active users, deterministic ties/cutoffs, recorded algorithm version, cache/snapshot integrity and abuse/suspension reconciliation. Paid placements, Business plan and founder status must not affect organic score. Existing popularity/featured lists remain separately labeled, unmodified until a migration is approved.

Comments require bounded plain text/sanitized rendering, verified active authors, own-edit/delete policy and history, reporting/moderation queue, moderator capabilities/revocation audit, spam/challenge/rate controls enforced beyond UI, protected emails/IP data and public-safe projections. Founder responses require verified ownership, not email-domain assertion/payment/receipt alone. Shared signout/redirect/opt-in and Business nonregression tests are required before reusing Supabase Auth. No A0 schema/API exists for these features.

Scoped code/schema search found no AIBeat product-vote/comment/period-leaderboard implementation in N/F. Matches were external badge upvote links, unsubscribe feedback, article text or unconnected design. C docs/community-phase-0/community-feature-design.md and feature-flags-rollback.md are detailed **PROPOSED** designs; proposed flags/quotas/tables are not enabled runtime controls. Preserve that work as reference, not mandatory product expansion.
