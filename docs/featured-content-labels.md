# Featured Content Labels

AIBeat uses labels to help readers understand why and how content appears.

## Labels

| Label | Purchasable | Rule |
|---|---:|---|
| Standard Listing | No | A regular accepted directory listing. |
| Featured | Yes | A paid visibility or presentation label. Does not imply editorial endorsement. |
| Spotlight | Yes | Promotional visibility. Must not imply independent editorial endorsement. |
| New Launch | Yes | Depends on verified launch timing or accepted launch package. |
| Sponsored | Yes | Required for paid editorial or paid promotional placements. |
| Partner | No | Indicates a real partnership or exchange approved by AIBeat. |
| Editor's Pick | No | Editorial-only label. It must not be purchasable. |

## Rules

- Sponsored must appear on paid editorial or promotional placements.
- Editor's Pick must not be purchasable.
- Partner must indicate a real partnership.
- Spotlight must not imply independent editorial endorsement.
- New Launch should depend on verified launch timing.
- Badge or backlink verification for Free Listing confirms website control only. It is not an endorsement.
