# Sponsored Content Policy

AIBeat may publish sponsored or partner content when the topic is useful, relevant, and clearly disclosed.

## Core Rules

- Sponsored content is labeled visibly.
- Partner content is labeled visibly.
- Affiliate relationships are disclosed where used.
- Payment does not guarantee positive editorial opinion.
- Payment does not buy Editor's Pick, rankings, review scores, or hidden recommendations.
- AIBeat may reject misleading, unsafe, irrelevant, or unverifiable products.
- Claims must be verifiable.
- AIBeat retains formatting and compliance control.

## Article Process

1. Sponsor or partner sends topic, product, audience, claims, assets, and preferred timing.
2. AIBeat reviews fit and disclosure requirements.
3. AIBeat confirms format, scope, revision policy, and timing.
4. Article is drafted or formatted according to AIBeat standards.
5. One revision round is included when specified in the package.
6. Article is published only after approval and disclosure checks.

## Link Policy

The current configurable policy is:

```text
allowFollowedSponsoredLinks=false
```

Do not promise do-follow sponsored backlinks unless this policy changes and the editorial policy explicitly allows it.

## Timing

Publication timing begins after assets, claims, disclosure label, and approval are complete.

## Rejection

AIBeat may reject:

- unsafe products;
- misleading claims;
- fake testimonials or fake metrics;
- unsupported ranking requests;
- hidden affiliate requests;
- content created only to manipulate search rankings.
