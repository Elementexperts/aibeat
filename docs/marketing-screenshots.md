# Marketing Screenshots

## Required Screenshots

Capture real UI screenshots after deployment or local preview:

1. Homepage hero and discovery.
2. Trending AI tools.
3. Tool categories.
4. AI news section.
5. Individual tool page.
6. Launch or Spotlight page.

## Viewports

- Mobile: `375px`
- Tablet: `768px`
- Desktop: `1280px`
- Wide desktop: `1440px`

## Recommended Dimensions

- Open Graph: `1200x630`
- Product Hunt gallery: `1270x760`
- LinkedIn feed: `1200x627`
- LinkedIn banner: `1584x396`
- Newsletter promo: `1200x675`
- Website marketplace gallery: `1600x1000`

## Review Checklist

- No personal data.
- No admin or automation interfaces.
- No fake metrics or testimonials.
- No broken images.
- Text readable at thumbnail size.
- Header, CTA, and primary section visible.
- Mobile screenshots do not have overlapping sticky UI.
