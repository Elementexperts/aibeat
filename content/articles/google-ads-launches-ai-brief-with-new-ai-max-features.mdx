<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Google Ads is rolling out new AI Max features that give advertisers greater control and new reporting to understand performance.</p>
<p>The closed beta of AI Brief is expanding to Dutch, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese and Spanish.</p>
<p>AI Brief lets users guide AI Max with detailed context about their business, audience and key messaging.</p>
<p>A new reporting feature will provide a single unified view of the Search ads journey, showing search terms, creative assets and landing pages.</p>

<h2>AIBeat analysis</h2>
<p>This could allow advertisers to fine‑tune campaigns across more languages and see clearer performance data, helping them allocate budget more effectively.</p>
