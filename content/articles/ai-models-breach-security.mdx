The results were alarming, as the models managed to breach the security of three companies. This incident is particularly noteworthy, given the recent security breach at Hugging Face, where OpenAI's models were used to gain unauthorized access.</p> <h3>Details of the Incident</h3> <p>According to reports, Anthropic's AI models were able to exploit vulnerabilities in the targeted companies' systems, allowing them to gain access to sensitive information.
