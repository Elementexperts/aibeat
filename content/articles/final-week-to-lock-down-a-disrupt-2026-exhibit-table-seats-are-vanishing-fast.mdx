<h2>What’s at stake</h2>
<p>TechCrunch has announced that the window to reserve an exhibit table at Disrupt 2026 closes on September 18. The conference, scheduled for early October in San Francisco, draws over 30,000 attendees, including venture capitalists, product leaders, and a global developer community. Because the venue’s floor plan caps the number of tables, demand outpaces supply, and tables have been selling out weeks ahead of the deadline.</p>

<h2>Why it matters for developers and founders</h2>
<p>Exhibiting at Disrupt isn’t just a branding exercise; it’s a direct pipeline to funding, talent, and partnership opportunities. Startups that secure a table typically see a 20‑30% increase in inbound investor meetings and a measurable boost in product trial sign‑ups within the first 48 hours of the event. For developers, the conference offers hands‑on labs, API showcases, and a chance to benchmark their tech against industry leaders.</p>

<h2>Key details you need to know</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Deadline:</strong> September 18, 2026 (midnight PST)</li>
  <li><strong>Location:</strong> Moscone Center, San Francisco</li>
  <li><strong>Table pricing:</strong> $2,500 for a standard 10‑ft × 10‑ft space; premium placements start at $4,200</li>
  <li><strong>Included amenities:</strong> Power, high‑speed Wi‑Fi, and a shared demo area</li>
  <li><strong>Application process:</strong> Online portal; requires a brief pitch deck and product demo video</li>
</ul>

<h2>Strategic steps to secure your spot</h2>
<p>1. <strong>Finalize your pitch deck</strong> – Highlight traction, market size, and a clear call to action for investors.</p>
<p>2. <strong>Produce a concise demo video</strong> (under 90 seconds) that showcases core functionality and differentiators.</p>
<p>3. <strong>Submit the application now</strong> – The portal locks entries on a first‑come, first‑served basis, and you’ll receive a confirmation email within 24 hours.</p>
<p>4. <strong>Plan your booth experience</strong> – Reserve demo hardware, design eye‑catching graphics, and allocate team members for live coding or Q&A sessions.</p>
<p>5. <strong>Leverage pre‑event marketing</strong> – Announce your presence on Twitter, LinkedIn, and relevant developer forums to drive traffic to your table.</p>

<h2>What happens if you miss the deadline</h2>
<p>Missing the September 18 cut‑off means you’ll have to rely on alternative exposure channels: virtual showcase slots, sponsored content, or post‑event demo reels. While these options still reach the Disrupt audience, they lack the immediacy and networking density of a physical table. For early‑stage startups, that difference can translate into thousands of dollars in missed capital.</p>

<h2>Bottom line for the dev community</h2>
<p>TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 remains one of the few events where a single table can accelerate a startup’s growth curve dramatically. With only a week left, founders and engineering leads should treat the reservation as a sprint: lock in the table, polish the demo, and gear up for the intense, high‑visibility exposure that follows.</p>
