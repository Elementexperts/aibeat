<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Supertake announced a seed financing of $20 million, a round led by venture firm Adjacent. The investment also included participation from Coinbase Ventures and Union Square Ventures, building on a prior $2 million pre‑seed round.</p>
<p>The company offers an AI‑powered platform that lets users translate market ideas into investment portfolios. It currently works with the brokerages Coinbase and Robinhood, and access remains limited to a waitlist while the service is in beta. Co‑founders Lincoln Murr (CEO) and John Peterson (CTO) oversee day‑to‑day operations.</p>

<h2>AIBeat analysis</h2>
<p>This funding could accelerate the rollout of Supertake&#39;s AI agents, potentially lowering the barrier for retail investors to build diversified portfolios. Integration with major brokerages such as Coinbase and Robinhood suggests the platform meets compliance and technical standards, while the waitlist indicates demand exceeds current capacity, hinting at a need for scaling before broader release.</p>
