<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Legato, a hearing technology company founded in 2024, emerged from stealth with <strong>$12 million in funding from Neotribe Ventures, Listen, and Village Global</strong>. The company also introduced its first product, <strong>Legato Frames</strong>, which Legato describes as the world’s first AI hearing glasses.</p>

<p>According to the company, Legato Frames integrate patented hearing-assistance technology into the arms of eyewear frames, combining hearing support with the familiar form factor of everyday glasses. Legato says the product is designed to address common barriers to traditional hearing aids, including cost, discomfort, and stigma.</p>

<h2>Why it matters</h2>
<p>Legato is approaching hearing assistance as both a technology and product-design problem. Instead of trying to make hearing devices disappear, the company is building hearing assistance into eyewear that people may already be comfortable wearing every day.</p>

<p>The company says its technology is protected by <strong>four granted patents and more than 20 pending applications</strong> and delivers performance that rivals traditional hearing aids. Legato was founded by industry veterans who previously worked on products at Bose and Apple.</p>

<p>The broader opportunity is significant because hearing loss affects millions of adults, while many people who could benefit from hearing assistance do not use traditional hearing aids. Legato’s approach highlights a growing convergence of AI, wearable hardware, accessibility, healthcare, and consumer design.</p>

<h2>Availability and pricing</h2>
<p>Legato says Frames will be available <strong>later this fall</strong> in a variety of styles, both online through the company’s website and through select eye care providers around the United States.</p>

<p>The company has not announced an exact retail price. Instead, Legato says Frames will be priced at a fraction of the cost of traditional hearing aids and will often be eligible for vision insurance when purchased through an eye care clinic.</p>

<h2>Looking ahead</h2>
<p>Legato’s launch is an example of how AI-enabled products are moving beyond standalone software into consumer hardware and accessibility-focused devices. The key question will be whether combining hearing assistance with a familiar eyewear form factor can make hearing technology easier for more people to adopt.</p>
