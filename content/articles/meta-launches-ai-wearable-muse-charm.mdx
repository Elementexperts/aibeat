<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Meta announced an AI wearable called Muse Charm, revealed by CEO Mark Zuckerberg at the company&#39;s Connect event. The palm-sized device can be carried in a pocket or attached to a keychain and includes a tiny screen that shows the avatar Jolly, representing the Muse AI agent. Users can speak to the device through a fingerprint sensor without unlocking a phone.</p>
<p>The product is not yet ready to ship, but Meta expects it to be available in time for the December holidays.</p>

<h2>AIBeat analysis</h2>
<p>If Muse Charm becomes available for the holiday season, it could give early adopters a new way to interact with Meta&#39;s AI services without needing a smartphone, potentially expanding the ecosystem of wearable AI.</p>
