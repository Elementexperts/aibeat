<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Xbox has secured exclusive streaming rights for Grand Theft Auto VI, meaning that at launch in November only Xbox Cloud Gaming will be able to stream the title.</p>
<p>Microsoft has not disclosed how long the exclusivity will last, and it will impose streaming hour limits of 15 hours for Game Pass Ultimate, 10 for Premium and 5 for Essential subscribers starting in November. A pay‑as‑you‑go option for Xbox Cloud Gaming is also planned for the following month.</p>

<p>This could restrict players on competing platforms from accessing the game via cloud until the exclusivity ends, potentially influencing subscription choices for gamers who prioritize early access to high‑profile releases.</p>
