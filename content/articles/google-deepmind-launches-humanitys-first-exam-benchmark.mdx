<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Google DeepMind announced the Humanity’s First Exam (HFE) benchmark on Kaggle, a dataset of 544 elementary text and image prompts that test 20 foundational capabilities and gauge how reliably large language models answer straightforward everyday questions. The launch was made by Oran Kelly, a research scientist at DeepMind, and a three‑week Kaggle competition runs through December 15, with a live leaderboard and technical report available.</p>

<h2>Progress to date</h2>
<p>Frontier models have shown significant progress on the HFE benchmark over the last six months.</p>

<h2>AIBeat analysis</h2>
<p>This could help developers gauge model reliability for everyday tasks, suggesting that improvements measured on HFE may translate to more trustworthy AI applications for end users.</p>
