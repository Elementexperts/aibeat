<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Tidal announced it will start banning AI‑generated music on December 9th.</p>
<p>The policy will block distributors from submitting songs that are solely AI‑generated, requiring the use of the Ircam Amplify detection tool, while allowing tracks that use AI only as an assistive tool.</p>
<p>The service estimates it will delete around 29 million AI‑generated songs and expects to reject an estimated 80,000 to 85,000 such tracks each day.</p>
<p>AI‑generated music comprised 43% of the tracks distributed to Tidal in the last 90 days, yet 96% of those songs receive zero plays on the platform.</p>

<h2>AIBeat analysis</h2>
<p>This could clean up Tidal’s catalog by removing a large volume of unheard AI‑only tracks, potentially improving discoverability for artists whose work receives actual streams and reducing server load associated with storing unused files.</p>
