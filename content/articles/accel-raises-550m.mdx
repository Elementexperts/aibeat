This development comes just 19 months after the firm's last India fund, which was valued at $650 million. Notably, Accel still has more than 55% of its previous fund available for deployment, indicating a sustained commitment to investing in the Indian startup ecosystem.</p><h3>Why This Matters</h3><p>The closure of this new fund is significant for several reasons. Firstly, it underscores the growing interest of international investors in India's tech industry.
