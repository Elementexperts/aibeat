<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Fujifilm announced the Instax Mini Evo 2 instant camera. It uses a 1/2.77‑inch sensor, larger than the first Mini Evo’s 1/5‑inch sensor, and includes a 4.5‑inch rear touchscreen display. The device can record short, silent MP4 video clips at 560 × 992 pixels and 24 FPS to a microSD card, charges via USB‑C, and prints 2 × 3‑inch Instax Mini photos. The Mini Evo 2 is priced at $290 and will be available in black only at the end of November.</p>

<h2>AIBeat analysis</h2>
<p>The larger sensor could deliver sharper, higher‑resolution stills compared with the original Mini Evo, which may attract users seeking better image quality while retaining the instant‑print experience. The addition of a touchscreen and video capability expands creative options, suggesting the camera could appeal to a broader range of casual photographers.</p>
