<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Reddit users have described shadows and visible LED strips around the edges of the screen on new Kindles, a phenomenon that becomes especially noticeable when dark mode is enabled. The reports cover both plastic and aluminum versions in every colour offered, indicating the issue is not limited to a single model type.</p>

<h2>Amazon&#39;s response</h2>
<p>Amazon confirmed it is aware of the screen light‑leak problem and indicated that a manufacturing problem may have introduced defects into the current batch of Kindles.</p>

<h2>AIBeat analysis</h2>
<p>This could affect reading comfort for users who rely on dark mode, as the edge lighting may be distracting. The acknowledgement of a manufacturing defect suggests that affected devices might be eligible for repair or replacement, prompting owners to contact support for resolution.</p>
