<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Microsoft started testing a suite of new customization options for Xbox consoles on November 12, 2026, making them available to members of the Xbox Insiders program. The initial features let Insiders try rotating background images and a shuffle function that automatically changes those backgrounds.</p>
<p>Insiders can save preferred dynamic backgrounds and choose how often the shuffle changes—at sign‑in, hourly, daily or weekly. They also have the option to move the Xbox Guide menu from its default left side to the right‑hand side of the screen.</p>
<p>For players who have claimed a digital license from a disc‑based game, the new update creates a separate library category for those titles and allows the digital license icon on game tiles to be hidden. Microsoft says the features will be expanded to more Insiders and eventually to the broader player base over time.</p>

<h2>AIBeat analysis</h2>
<p>These changes could give Xbox users more control over the look and feel of their console interface, making the experience feel more personal and potentially increasing engagement with the system. If the customization tools prove popular among Insiders, Microsoft may incorporate them into a wider update, affecting how all Xbox owners organize their game libraries and interact with the Guide menu.</p>
