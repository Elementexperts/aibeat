<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Nous Research announced a $90 million Series B financing round that values the company at $1.5 billion.</p>
<p>The round was led by Robot Ventures and included investors Nvidia, Union Square Ventures, Menlo Ventures, Samsung and 1789 Capital, bringing total funding to $158 million.</p>
<p>The capital will be used to launch Hermes for Businesses, a service that lets companies deploy customized AI agents for multi‑step workflows while keeping data private and secure.</p>
<p>The open‑source Hermes Agent has been cloned over 24 million times and accounts for roughly 2.5 % of global AI token usage, according to the startup.</p>
<p>By mid‑September 2026 the company reported about $36 million in annualized revenue and expects to exceed $100 million before the end of the year.</p>
