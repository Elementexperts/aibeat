<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Meta released a patch for its Muse macOS app addressing a zero‑day vulnerability that could allow attackers to take control of the AI agent.</p>
<p>The flaw, discovered by researcher Patrick Wardle, exploited an undocumented Muse setting that let local code redirect transcription processing to an attacker‑controlled endpoint.</p>
<p>Meta clarified the exploit required local access, not remote, making the practical risk to typical users low.</p>

<h2>AIBeat analysis</h2>
<p>This could mean that users who apply the update are protected from local privilege escalation, while devices running unpatched versions may remain vulnerable to attackers with local access.</p>
