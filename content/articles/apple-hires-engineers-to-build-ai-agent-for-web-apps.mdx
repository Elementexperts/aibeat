<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Apple announced it is hiring engineers to develop its own AI agent. The role will involve close collaboration with Siri, UI Frameworks, and Apple Foundation Models divisions.</p>
<p>The planned agent is described as capable of browsing the web, working across apps, writing code, and calling external services. Apple also intends to give Siri new agentic abilities such as rotating and cropping photos and organizing Safari tabs.</p>

<h2>AIBeat analysis</h2>
<p>If the new agent integrates these capabilities, it could extend Siri’s functionality beyond voice commands, allowing users to perform more complex tasks directly within apps, which may influence how developers design app interactions.</p>
