<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Warhound, a Texas‑based defense startup founded by virtual‑reality pioneer Palmer Luckey and entrepreneur Ben Lamm, announced its new canine program.</p>
<p>The company has raised $200 million in funding from investors to develop the project.</p>
<p>Warhound intends to partner with Colossal Biosciences to apply genetics, cloning and animal‑biology techniques to create dogs with enhanced speed, agility and olfactory abilities, drawing on breeds such as Belgian Malinois and German Shepherd.</p>
<p>The dogs will be fitted with Anduril’s cybernetic Augments, which include eye and ear protection and EagleEye sensor suites.</p>
<p>Marine veteran and intelligence specialist David Spirk serves as CEO, while the American Humane Society will advise the program and certify training protocols. Palmer Luckey holds no equity or ongoing role in the venture.</p>
<p>The initiative seeks to lessen U.S. reliance on Europe, which currently supplies about 80 percent of military working dogs, and envisions the enhanced canines serving elite roles for the armed forces, emergency responders and law‑enforcement agencies.</p>
