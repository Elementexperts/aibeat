<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Google introduced three new capabilities to Gemini Notebook. The Short Video Overviews feature creates a 60‑second video summary of notebook content.</p>
<p>The Interactive Learning Overviews, placed under Reports, bundle summaries, slides, quizzes and flashcards, while the quiz engine now supports short‑answer, multi‑select and fill‑in‑the‑blank questions. Users can also request feedback after completing self‑tests or reviewing flashcards.</p>

<h2>AIBeat analysis</h2>
<p>These additions could help students and professionals streamline review sessions by providing quick visual summaries and varied assessment formats, and the feedback option may guide further study focus.</p>
