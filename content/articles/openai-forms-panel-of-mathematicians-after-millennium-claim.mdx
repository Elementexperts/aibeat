<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>OpenAI announced the creation of an independent panel of mathematicians to advise the company on its interactions with the mathematical research community. The company also said it had solved one of the seven Millennium Prize problems.</p>

<h2>AIBeat analysis</h2>
<p>If the claim of solving a Millennium problem is verified, it could boost OpenAI’s reputation in the scientific community and justify the new advisory panel, suggesting the company is seeking structured input to manage the impact of such breakthroughs on research practices.</p>
