<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>OpenAI&#39;s quarterly revenue crossed $10 billion for the first time.</p>
<p>The $10 billion figure represents an almost 50% increase from the $6.7 billion reported in the second quarter.</p>
<p>OpenAI&#39;s adjusted operating margin narrowed from -150% in the second quarter to -109% in the third quarter.</p>
<p>Bloomberg reported that OpenAI&#39;s annual revenue run rate reached $50 billion.</p>

<h2>AIBeat analysis</h2>
<p>This could indicate that OpenAI&#39;s growth trajectory is accelerating, suggesting that its services are gaining broader commercial adoption, though the still‑negative operating margin shows the company continues to invest heavily in development and infrastructure.</p>
