<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Donald Trump announced a proposal to impose a tremendous tax on companies that build data centers outside the United States. The announcement was made at a press conference on Friday after his summit with Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin, and he was flanked by Vice President JD Vance and Secretary of State Marco Rubio during the remarks.</p>

<h2>AIBeat analysis</h2>
<p>This could encourage technology firms to keep new data infrastructure within the United States, potentially boosting domestic investment and reducing reliance on foreign facilities. The policy suggests a strategic effort to limit overseas data center growth that could benefit competitors such as China.</p>
