<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Dario Amodei, the chief executive of Anthropic, described accusations that the company intends to surrender human free will to artificial intelligence as absurd.</p>
<p>In a written statement he argued that society should never sacrifice legitimacy or self‑determination for efficiency or security, and he warned that turning over governance to AI would be a poor choice. He also clarified that allowing AI to automate specific tasks such as driving, drug design, or software development is distinct from relinquishing free will.</p>
<p>Amodei suggested that a line should be drawn at recursive self‑improvement, where AI improves itself without human help, and he noted that Anthropic does not expect its model Claude to build frontier models fully autonomously without meaningful human supervision.</p>

<h2>AIBeat analysis</h2>
<p>This could mean Anthropic will continue to embed strong human oversight into Claude’s development pipeline, reinforcing a stance that AI systems should augment rather than replace human decision‑making.</p>
