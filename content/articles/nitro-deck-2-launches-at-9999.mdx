<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>The Nitro Deck 2 is now available for $99.99 and works with both the original Nintendo Switch and the Switch 2.</p>

<h2>Features</h2>
<p>The accessory includes a twisting mechanism on its back that can be loosened, tightened, or locked.</p>
<p>It also offers adjustable tension on both sticks, letting users modify snap‑back speed.</p>
<p>Reviewers note that the stick‑to‑button arrangement is too close together for comfortable use.</p>
