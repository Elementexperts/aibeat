<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Sony announced it is canceling Fairgames, a live‑service shooter that was first announced in 2023.</p>
<p>The decision follows mixed player feedback and retention results that fell short of internal goals, and Sony cited challenging market conditions in the PvP shooter genre.</p>
<p>Sony said the cancellation will not lead to job cuts, and developer Haven Studios, which Sony acquired in 2022, will now support other PlayStation studios.</p>

<h2>AIBeat analysis</h2>
<p>This could signal Sony’s shift away from launching new PvP shooters, focusing instead on leveraging Haven Studios to bolster existing PlayStation titles, which may affect how the company allocates resources in a competitive market.</p>
