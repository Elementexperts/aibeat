<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Google released a Home app update that adds its Familiar Faces AI feature to two Onn security cameras: the $35 Outdoor Camera Plug‑In and the $77 Floodlight Camera Hardwired.</p>
<p>The feature uses artificial intelligence to recognize familiar people and is available only to Google Home Premium subscribers.</p>
<p>The update also adds a remote‑control button in the app for both Onn models and for Google’s Nest Cam Outdoor (wired, 2nd‑gen), allowing users to start or stop a 30‑second security siren while watching live video.</p>

<h2>AIBeat analysis</h2>
<p>This could let homeowners more easily identify known visitors and quickly trigger a deterrent siren, but the requirement for a paid Premium subscription may limit adoption among price‑sensitive buyers.</p>
