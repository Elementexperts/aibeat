<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>TechCrunch held its Founder Summit 2026 at the SoWa Power Station in Boston.</p>
<p>Doors opened at 9 a.m. ET, and the registration and expo area remained open until 4:30 p.m. ET.</p>
<p>The Networking Lounge, powered by Braindate, operated from 9:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. ET, while breakout sessions ran from 10:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. ET, followed by a closing reception from 4:30 p.m. to 6:30 p.m. ET.</p>
<p>Attendees who registered before 9 a.m. ET saved up to $100 on a pass and received a second pass at no additional cost.</p>
<p>Speakers included Steve Fredette of Toast and Amulya Vishwanath of Techable Ventures.</p>
