<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Google introduced the Guided Vision feature for Gemini Live on compatible Android devices. The tool provides real‑time audio descriptions of anything the phone’s camera points at and is designed for people who are blind or have low vision. Users can ask follow‑up questions, such as reading an item’s expiration date, and the feature is also accessible through Google TalkBack and an accessibility shortcut on Android 9 plus devices.</p>
<p>Google advises that Guided Vision should not be used for navigation, safe‑travel guidance, obstacle detection, or as a replacement for a cane or other mobility aid.</p>

<h2>AIBeat analysis</h2>
<p>This could give blind and low‑vision users immediate auditory information about objects they point their phone at, expanding the ways they can identify items without assistance; however, because Google warns against relying on the feature for navigation or obstacle detection, users will still need traditional mobility aids for safe travel.</p>
