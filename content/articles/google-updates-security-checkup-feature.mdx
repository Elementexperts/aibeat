<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Google published an announcement on Oct 30, 2026 describing its Security Checkup feature, which shows who has access to a Gmail inbox.</p>
<p>The checkup can be completed in about two minutes and guides users through three steps: reviewing signed‑in devices, removing old third‑party app access, and confirming recovery information.</p>

<h2>AIBeat analysis</h2>
<p>This could allow Gmail users to quickly identify and remove unauthorized access, potentially strengthening account security by making routine reviews easy and fast.</p>
