<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>A segment of Repentance Plus contained music that was generated by artificial intelligence, a fact uncovered when a Reddit user traced Suno metadata in the game files. Edmund McMillen said he had no idea AI music was used in the latest version. Valve’s policy requires developers to disclose AI usage, and Nicalis had not done so.</p>
<p>The undisclosed AI tracks triggered a wave of negative reviews on Steam, with the game receiving an overwhelmingly negative rating. In response Nicalis issued an update that removed the generated music files from Repentance Plus.</p>
