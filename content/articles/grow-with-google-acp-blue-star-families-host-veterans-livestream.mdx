<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Grow with Google, American Corporate Partners and Blue Star Families are hosting a free livestream for National Veterans Small Business Week. It is the third annual livestream and is scheduled for Monday, November 9, 2026 at 1:00 p.m. Eastern Time, and will be broadcast on YouTube, offering practical AI skills for veteran and military‑spouse entrepreneurs.</p>
<p>The event includes a veteran entrepreneur spotlight featuring Adam Baker, founder and CEO of the veteran‑owned pet toy company SodaPup.</p>
<p>A live Q&amp;A session with representatives from ACP and Blue Star Families will follow, and attendees can sign up for a complimentary 30‑minute one‑on‑one virtual coaching session with a Grow with Google Coach after the event.</p>
<p>Registration for the livestream is available at goo.gle/NVSBW26.</p>
