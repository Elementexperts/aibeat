<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Amazon agreed to acquire Decart for $7.5 billion.</p>
<p>The transaction will bring the Decart Optimization Stack (DOS) technology and Decart’s founding team and AI researchers into Amazon’s portfolio.</p>
<p>At the same time, Amazon is preparing to start selling its Trainium chips to customers.</p>

<h2>AIBeat analysis</h2>
<p>This could strengthen Amazon’s AI hardware and software ecosystem by pairing the DOS optimization software with its own Trainium chips, potentially delivering better performance and efficiency for cloud‑based AI workloads.</p>
