<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Google Photos introduced Photos in Gemini Spark, an AI feature that lets users select, brighten and share family portraits with a single prompt.</p>
<p>A new wardrobe feature automatically catalogs clothing seen in images and offers a virtual try‑on experience for styling outfits.</p>
<p>Moods editing styles add nostalgic, vintage‑inspired looks such as 35mm film and 2000s digicam filters.</p>
<p>The update includes 15 new Remix templates that help users create birthday cards, photo‑booth shots and other creative images.</p>
<p>Google Photos upgraded its markup tool with a redact pen, precise thickness sliders and additional fonts for adding text.</p>

<h2>AIBeat analysis</h2>
<p>These tools could streamline photo editing and sharing, allowing users to generate polished portraits, experiment with personal style and produce custom graphics without external apps, which suggests a more integrated creative workflow within the platform.</p>
