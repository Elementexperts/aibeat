<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Daniel Ek is backing Europa Fusion with funding, marking a high‑profile investment in the startup&#39;s fusion ambitions.</p>

<h2>Company background</h2>
<p>Europa Fusion is a stealthy fusion power startup founded by an AI researcher in February, with Nantas Nardelli as its founder.</p>
<p>Nardelli previously worked at Google DeepMind, Facebook AI Research, and Microsoft Research Cambridge, and served as head of machine learning at Pacific Fusion, which has broken ground on a demonstration facility in New Mexico.</p>

<h2>Technology focus</h2>
<p>The startup will pursue a pulsed‑power approach to fusion, using massive, precisely timed surges of electrical current to compress fuel targets until atoms fuse and release energy.</p>

<p>This could accelerate private development of compact fusion energy by signaling confidence in pulsed‑power methods, and it suggests that backing from prominent tech investors may draw additional capital to similar high‑risk energy projects.</p>
