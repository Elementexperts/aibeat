<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Amazon announced its first smart lock that can be charged by turning a dial when the battery dies, incorporating a charger into its thumb‑turn dial.</p>
<p>The lock will be priced at $249 and is expected to be available in the US in early 2027 pending FCC approval.</p>
<p>Unlocking options include a fingerprint reader and a backlit touchscreen keypad with multiple user registration and scheduling.</p>
<p>The lock can be opened, locked, and monitored using the Ring mobile app or Alexa‑compatible voice commands.</p>
<p>Installation uses Ring’s replaceable Quick Release Ultra Battery Pack and requires no wiring.</p>
<p>The lock connects wirelessly via Amazon’s Sidewalk network instead of Wi‑Fi and can be paired with a Ring smart doorbell for visual monitoring, though it does not include a camera.</p>

<h2>AIBeat analysis</h2>
<p>This could reduce the hassle of battery replacement for users, because the built‑in charger lets the lock be recharged simply by turning the thumb‑turn dial, meaning homeowners may maintain security without regular battery swaps.</p>
<p>However, the lock’s rollout depends on FCC approval, so consumers may need to wait until early 2027 before they can purchase the device.</p>
