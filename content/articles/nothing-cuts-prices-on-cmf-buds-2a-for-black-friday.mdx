<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Black‑color CMF Buds 2A are listed at $17.89 on Amazon for Black Friday, roughly $11 below the manufacturer’s suggested retail price. The light‑gray and orange versions are priced at $19.99, representing a $9 discount off their regular price. All three color options—black, light gray and orange—are offered for the model.</p>
<p>The earbuds provide active noise cancellation, support Google Fast Pair and deliver up to eight hours of playback from the included USB‑C charging case. They carry an IP54 rating for water and dust resistance and include customizable equalizer settings as well as multipoint connectivity for pairing with multiple devices.</p>
