<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Wyze released a new version of its Ultra BodyScan smart scale called the Ultra BodyScan Plus, moving the 4.3‑inch customizable color screen to a retractable handle so the display sits at eye level.</p>
<p>The scale is priced at $149 and is available through Wyze’s online store in black or white, about $30 higher than the prior Ultra BodyScan model that cost $119.98.</p>
<p>It now measures over 50 body composition metrics, a jump from the 13 metrics offered by the earlier version, using bioelectrical impedance analysis with a conductive indium tin oxide coated glass surface that works regardless of foot placement.</p>
<p>Data can sync to Apple Health, Google Health and Android Health Connect via Bluetooth or directly through Wi‑Fi cloud, and the device does not require a subscription fee.</p>
<p>The scale can store health metrics for up to eight registered users and unlimited guest measurements, and includes a mode for weighing children, pets and luggage without advanced health metrics.</p>

<p>This could make detailed health tracking more accessible for households that need to monitor several people, since the device supports multiple users, guest entries and a simple weight‑only mode, all without ongoing fees; the expanded metric set and higher price suggest Wyze is positioning the scale as a more comprehensive health hub for families and fitness enthusiasts.</p>
