<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Meta announced its new AI agent Muse at the annual Connect event. Muse is a consumer‑focused, cute, Tamagotchi‑style AI agent intended for adults only. The agent had been available for a couple of weeks at the time of reporting. Meta plans to push AI features everywhere across its platforms.</p>

<h2>Features</h2>
<p>Muse can scan for unclaimed funds and could help with financial tasks such as canceling subscriptions or identifying double charges, similar to services like Rocket Money. A tester reported that Muse found unclaimed money for him, describing the capability as a party‑trick feature.</p>

<h2>Concerns</h2>
<p>Some observers worry that users may be reluctant to share sensitive financial data with Meta’s AI, given the company’s reliance on advertising revenue.</p>

<h2>AIBeat analysis</h2>
<p>This could make financial management more convenient for users who adopt Muse, but the trust issue highlighted in the concerns suggests adoption may depend on how Meta addresses data privacy concerns.</p>
