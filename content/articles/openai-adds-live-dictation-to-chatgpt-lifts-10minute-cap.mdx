<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>OpenAI has introduced a Live Dictation feature for ChatGPT that transcribes audio prompts as users speak.</p>
<p>The update eliminates the earlier 10‑minute recording restriction on voice dictation.</p>
<p>Live Dictation also delivers faster transcription of spoken input compared with prior methods.</p>
<p>The feature is being rolled out worldwide on all ChatGPT plans across the web interface, desktop application, and iOS and Android mobile apps.</p>
<p>It is currently in beta and available only to Plus, Enterprise, Edu, Business, and Pro workspaces.</p>
<p>Users can tap or click the audio waveform to view the live text transcript and edit it directly in the prompt box.</p>

<h2>AIBeat analysis</h2>
<p>Removing the 10‑minute cap and providing faster transcription could let users rely more on spoken input for longer or more complex queries, potentially streamlining workflows for professionals who prefer voice over typing.</p>
