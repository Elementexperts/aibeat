<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>BYD announced a flagship purple electric minivan called the BYD Whale.</p>
<p>The Whale is estimated to travel 559 miles (900 km) on a charge, measures 17.3 feet long, 6.5 feet wide and 6 feet tall with a 123‑inch wheelbase, and can seat seven passengers while carrying seven suitcases, making it larger than a Honda Odyssey, Chrysler Pacifica, and Toyota Sienna.</p>
<p>The vehicle will be equipped with LiDAR for advanced hands‑free driving, and BYD claims its battery can charge from 10% to 97% in nine minutes and from 10% to 70% in five minutes; a plug‑in hybrid version offering a 248‑mile range will also be offered.</p>
<p>BYD plans to ship the Whale in China in the first quarter of 2027, with an expected price of around 300,000 yuan, roughly $45,000.</p>
