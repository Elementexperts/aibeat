<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Amazon has held talks about ending its block on Meta’s Muse AI shopping agent.</p>
<p>The discussions focus on the kinds of products Muse would recommend and the potential impact on Amazon’s advertising revenue.</p>
<p>Amazon is concerned that allowing Muse could cause loss of advertising revenue because it relies on humans seeing ads.</p>
<p>Amazon is worried about the quality of product search results Muse would present and its effect on customer relationships.</p>
<p>Amazon previously blocked Perplexity’s AI agent from shopping on its site and promotes its own Alexa for Shopping assistant.</p>

<h2>AIBeat analysis</h2>
<p>This could mean Amazon may weigh the trade‑off between opening its platform to a competitor’s AI and protecting its ad‑based revenue model, suggesting any policy shift will likely include safeguards on ad exposure and result quality.</p>
