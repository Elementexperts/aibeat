<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Ecoflow lowered the price of its Laptop Power Bank to $48.99 for Amazon Prime subscribers, a drop from the previous $85.79.</p>
<p>The power bank can output up to 140 W from each of its two USB‑C ports and supply a combined 170 W to up to three devices at once.</p>
<p>It houses a 25,000 mAh (90 Wh) battery, is TSA‑approved for air travel, and is available in black or silver.</p>
