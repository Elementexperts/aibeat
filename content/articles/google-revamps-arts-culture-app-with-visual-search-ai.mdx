<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Google has launched a redesign of its Arts &amp; Culture app that emphasizes visual‑first conversational search, interactive AI experiments, and a larger City Guide.</p>
<p>The update is being rolled out globally today and can be downloaded or updated on Google Play and the Apple App Store.</p>
<p>The redesign coincides with the app’s 15th anniversary.</p>

<h2>AIBeat analysis</h2>
<p>This could let users explore cultural content more intuitively, as the visual search lets them start from images and the AI experiments let them interact with historical figures, potentially increasing engagement with museum collections.</p>
<p>The expanded City Guide, adding 27 new destinations, could make the app more useful for travelers seeking cultural highlights, suggesting broader adoption beyond traditional museum visitors.</p>
