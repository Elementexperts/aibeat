<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Medium launched a new app called TK that allows users with a Medium account to request AI assistance by typing @TK followed by a prompt.</p>
<p>The app includes skills for editorial feedback, headline feedback, and matching drafts to Medium publication style guides, and it can import and export Markdown though it is not a Markdown editor.</p>
<p>TK is offered as a free tool and at launch is available on the web, with a desktop app planned for the future.</p>
<p>Writing in TK is not encrypted, but users can set access‑control permissions on their work, and the app can optionally connect to a user’s own AI agent via TK’s MCP server or Claude skill.</p>

<h2>AIBeat analysis</h2>
<p>This could let writers obtain immediate editorial and headline suggestions that align with publication style guides, potentially speeding up the drafting process; however, because writing in TK is not encrypted, authors may need to consider the sensitivity of the content they share, especially when connecting external AI agents.</p>
