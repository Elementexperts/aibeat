<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Google Arts &amp; Culture partnered with the James Beard Foundation to launch the James Beard Menu Archive, a digital collection of more than 10,000 historic menus. The archive uses artificial intelligence to examine ingredient usage over decades, noting trends such as the rise of kale in the 2000s and the decline of foie gras. It also features a curated story series that highlights James Beard and the history of the foundation’s New York City headquarters.</p>
<p>The James Beard Foundation has organized dinner events for over forty years, serving as a launchpad for many top chefs.</p>
<p>Award‑winning chef Marcus Samuelsson noted that the collaboration celebrates the people behind each plate and aims to inspire the next generation of culinary creatives.</p>
