<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Walmart announced a partnership with Meta to let shoppers access Black Friday deals via Meta’s AI agent Muse, a service that began in October and is slated for broader developer access.</p>
<p>Customers open the Muse app, sign in to their Walmart account and can ask Muse to locate items, compare options or monitor deals, with Walmart supplying relevant products and automatically building a shopping cart.</p>
<p>Muse does not retrieve a shopper’s Walmart purchase history or other personal data; users can set spending caps, require purchase approval and delete the agent’s profile at any time.</p>
<p>The integration runs on Walmart’s Agent Gateway, a platform that enables external AI assistants to connect directly to Walmart’s online store infrastructure.</p>

<h2>AIBeat analysis</h2>
<p>This could make Black Friday shopping faster and more convenient by allowing shoppers to use conversational AI to find and add items without navigating the website, while the built‑in privacy controls address concerns about data sharing between the retailer and the AI provider.</p>
