<h2>Introduction to the Problem</h2><p>Historian Jill Lepore recently appeared on the Equity podcast, discussing the tech industry's flawed understanding of science fiction and its implications for democracy. Lepore argues that the industry's leaders, such as Elon Musk, are <strong>bad science fiction readers</strong> who misinterpret the genre's themes and warnings.</p><h3>Government by Machines</h3><p>Lepore's concept of <strong>government by machines</strong> refers to the increasing reliance on technology to manage and govern society. This phenomenon is rooted in a misguided belief that machines can make better decisions than humans. However, Lepore contends that this approach undermines democracy and ignores the complexities of human nature.</p><h2>The Dangers of Misinterpretation</h2><p>The tech industry's misreading of science fiction has significant consequences. By ignoring the warnings and themes present in science fiction, developers and founders are creating technologies that <strong>erode democratic values</strong>. Lepore cites examples such as the rise of surveillance capitalism and the proliferation of disinformation, which are facilitated by technologies that prioritize efficiency and profit over human well-being.</p><h3>What Developers Can Do</h3><ul><li><strong>Rethink the role of technology in governance</strong>: Developers must consider the potential consequences of creating technologies that manage and govern society.</li><li><strong>Engage with science fiction critically</strong>: Developers should read and interpret science fiction with a critical eye, recognizing both its warnings and its potential to inspire positive change.</li><li><strong>Prioritize democratic values</strong>: Developers must prioritize democratic values, such as transparency, accountability, and human well-being, when designing and implementing technologies.</li></ul><h2>A Call to Action</h2><p>As the tech industry continues to shape and transform society, it is essential that developers and founders recognize the importance of <strong>responsible innovation</strong>.
