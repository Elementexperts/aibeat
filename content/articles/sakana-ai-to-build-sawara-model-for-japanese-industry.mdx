<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Sakana AI, a Japanese AI startup led by co‑founder and CEO David Ha, announced it will develop its own foundation model named Sawara. Development starts this month, with a smaller‑scale version slated for training and release in early 2027 and a larger version to follow, and the small model is designed for on‑premises deployment. The model is aimed at supporting Japan’s industrial and manufacturing sectors.</p>
<p>The company previously launched the AI orchestration system Fugu and created Namazu, a Japanese‑focused language model fine‑tuned from open‑weight models. It also signed a research contract with the Ministry of Defense to explore AI for multi‑source analysis and has worked with the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications on disinformation detection systems.</p>
<p>CEO David Ha said Sakana AI has no near‑term plans to go public and that its future growth is linked to the global expansion of Japanese manufacturers.</p>

<h2>AIBeat analysis</h2>
<p>This could give Japanese manufacturers domestic AI tools tailored to production needs, potentially reducing reliance on foreign models and enabling on‑premises deployment for sensitive data, as the Sawara model is intended for industrial use and the small version is suited for on‑site deployment.</p>
