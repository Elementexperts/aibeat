<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Anthropic was founded in 2021 with the purpose of ensuring humanity can control AI and is organized as a public benefit corporation.</p>
<p>The board is chaired by president Daniela Amodei and includes CEO Dario Amodei, Reed Hastings, Christopher Liddell, Vas Narasimhan, and Yasmin Razavi, with one vacancy.</p>
<p>Control of the board is allocated to a Long Term Benefit Trust (LTBT) chaired by Neil Shah, which also includes Ben Bernanke, Richard Fontaine, and Christopher Stubbs, with one vacancy.</p>
<p>Anthropic’s founders jointly own a special class of stock that gives them 50.1% of shareholder voting power, and the board can decline the LTBT’s nomination of a director and refer the decision to employees for a vote.</p>
<p>Ten years after the IPO, the founders’ control ends automatically if fewer than three founders remain; after that, the LTBT continues until 85% of common shareholders vote to end it, and if the special class of shareholder no longer exists, common shareholders gain control.</p>

<h2>AIBeat analysis</h2>
<p>This could allow Anthropic to prioritize long‑term safety goals while giving founders decisive early voting power; the board’s ability to involve employees in director nominations suggests a broader stakeholder role; the automatic sunset of founder control and the eventual shift to common shareholder governance could change decision‑making dynamics once the LTBT is dissolved, potentially affecting how future AI policies are set.</p>
