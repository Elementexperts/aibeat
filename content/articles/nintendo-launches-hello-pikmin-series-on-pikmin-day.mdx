<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Nintendo is celebrating the first annual Pikmin Day on November 1, 2026 by debuting a new series of short films called Hello! Pikmin. The series launches with its second episode on the day of the event and will add a third episode later in November followed by a new short each month starting in December.</p>
<p>Shigeru Miyamoto used the Pikmin Day Direct to reveal new details about Pikmin 4 for the upcoming Switch 2, including the ability for players to issue voice commands to the space dog Oatchi using the console’s built‑in microphone. He also announced a Dandori Academy mode that will test player skills through specific challenges, and said limited tests for the game will run on Friday, Saturday and Sunday with leaderboards for participants.</p>
<p>In addition to the new shorts, Miyamoto announced an update for the mobile app Pikmin Bloom that adds a feature called Secret Caves, allowing players to win treasure by battling enemies and exploring caves.</p>
