The investment also saw participation from Allianz, Rebel Fund, Brick and Mortar Ventures’ Darren Bechtel, and Y Combinator’s Garry Tan. This significant funding is a testament to the growing need for AI compliance solutions in the infrastructure sector.</p><h3>Why AI Compliance Matters</h3><p>As infrastructure projects increasingly leverage AI and machine learning, the need for effective compliance measures has never been more pressing. AI compliance is crucial to ensure that infrastructure projects meet regulatory requirements, mitigate risks, and maintain public trust.
