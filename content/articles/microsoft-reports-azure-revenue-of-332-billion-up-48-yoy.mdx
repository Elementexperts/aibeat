<h2>What happened</h2>
<p>Azure generated $33.2 billion in revenue in the July–September quarter, marking a 48 % year‑over‑year rise. This amount represented roughly 36 % of Microsoft’s total quarterly revenue of $92.3 billion. The figure also exceeded analyst expectations by about $850 million, which had projected $32.3 billion. Azure is part of Microsoft’s Agents and Infra segment, which reported $76.8 billion in revenue, up 25 % from a year earlier.</p>

<h2>AIBeat analysis</h2>
<p>The strong Azure performance could signal continued momentum for Microsoft’s cloud business, which now contributes a sizable share of overall earnings. If the growth trend persists, the segment may further lift Microsoft’s profitability and give the company more leverage in pricing and investment decisions. However, the reliance on Azure for over a third of total revenue also means that any slowdown in cloud spending could have a disproportionate impact on Microsoft’s quarterly results.</p>
